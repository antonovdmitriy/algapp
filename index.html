Made for Life in Sweden

Your Language.
Your Pace.

Whether you have just started SFI or need Swedish for work, Älg meets you in the language you already know.

When I moved to Sweden, I went through SFI myself. I looked for an app to practise words in between lessons and couldn't find a calm one — without ads, without pressure and without tricks to keep me hooked.

So I started building Älg in my spare time. I still make it on my own.

Stories

Read. Listen.
Understand.

101 short stories from everyday life in Sweden, from A1 to C1 — each one illustrated and read aloud by a Swedish narrator.

  • 📖

    Read at your level

    Stories are grouped by CEFR level, so you start with simple A1 texts about fika and a trip to the market and work your way up.

  • 🎧

    Listen at your pace

    A native-sounding narrator reads every story. Beginner levels are read a little slower, and you can set the speed for each level yourself — or hide the text and train your ear.

  • 👆

    Tap any word

    Don't know a word? Tap it to see the translation in your language and open the full word card with forms and examples.

  • ✅

    Check what you understood

    Every story ends with a short quiz of 3–4 questions. Answer them all correctly to mark the story as completed.

Ask anything about Swedish: why it's "en hund" and not "ett hund", the difference between "tala" and "prata", or what to focus on next. Älg AI knows your progress and answers in plain language.

FAQ

Questions & Answers

Is Älg free?

Yes. Älg is free to download and has no ads. All words, stories, audio, games and spaced review are free to use.

Which languages can I learn Swedish from?

Translations of every word and example are available in 21 languages: Arabic, Belarusian, Chinese, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Persian, Polish, Russian, Serbian, Somali, Spanish, Turkish and Ukrainian. The app's menus are translated into 14 of them. With Belarusian, Estonian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Persian, Serbian and Somali you see translations in your language and menus in English.

Does Älg help with SFI?

Älg is not an official SFI course, but it is made for the same goal: Swedish for everyday life in Sweden. Practise the words from your lessons, listen to native voices and read short stories at your level, from A1 to C1.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without an account. A free account backs up your progress, syncs it between devices and unlocks Älg AI.

Does it work offline?

Words, translations and stories are stored on your phone. Audio downloads the first time you play it and then stays on your device, so a word you have listened to once also plays without internet.

Is there an Android or iPad version?

Älg is made for iPhone and needs iOS 17 or later. There is no Android version yet.
