# Privacy Policy

Älg application does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- No personal data is collected and you are not tracked across apps or websites.
- The app uses [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/) for anonymous usage
  statistics: which screens and features are used, and how far onboarding gets. Signals
  carry no name, e-mail, account or advertising identifier, and nothing you type or study.
  The identifier TelemetryDeck receives is a hashed, anonymised value that cannot be traced
  back to you or your device.
- No other third-party services are integrated that collect personal data.
