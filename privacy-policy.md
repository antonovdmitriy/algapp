# Privacy Policy

Älg is a Swedish learning app. You can use all learning modes without an account; in
that case nothing that identifies you leaves your device. This page describes what is
collected in each case and why.

## Using the app without an account

Your progress — learned, favorite and ignored words, review schedule, completed
stories, daily activity and settings — is stored only on your device.

## Account and progress sync

Creating an account is optional. If you sign in (with Apple, Google or e-mail), we store:

- **Your e-mail address, name and an account identifier**, as provided by the sign-in
  method you chose. They are used only to sign you in and to show your account in the app.
- **Your learning progress**: the lists of learned, favorite and ignored words, review
  schedule, completed stories, daily activity history and app settings. It is stored
  under your account identifier so that it can be restored on another device.

Accounts and progress are stored with Microsoft Azure. We do not use this data for
advertising and do not share it with anyone else.

## Älg AI chat

The AI chat is available to signed-in users. When you send a message, the message, the
recent messages of the same conversation, a summary of your progress (for example how
many words you have learned and your streak) and, if you opened the chat from a word,
that word are sent to our server and passed on to Google's Gemini API to produce the
answer. Our server does not store the conversation; the chat history is kept on your
device. Please do not include personal information in chat messages.

## Anonymous usage statistics

The app uses [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/) to understand how it is
used: which screens and features are opened, how far onboarding gets, the translation
language and level you selected, and basic technical details such as app version and
device model. These signals carry no name, e-mail, account or advertising identifier,
and nothing you type or study. The identifier TelemetryDeck receives is a hashed,
anonymised value that we cannot trace back to you or connect to your account.

## Microphone and speech recognition

Pronunciation practice uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition, and only
when you start it. We do not receive or store the recordings.

## What we do not do

- No advertising and no tracking across other apps or websites.
- No sale of personal data.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app: open the Profile tab, tap your avatar and choose
**Delete Account**. The account and the progress stored with it are removed from our
servers right away and cannot be restored. Progress kept on the device stays there until
you delete the app.

If you cannot use the app, write to the address below from the e-mail of your account
and we will delete it for you.
